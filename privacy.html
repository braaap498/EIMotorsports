Privacy Policy

EIMotorsports Inventory. Last updated: October 2026.

EIMotorsports Inventory is a private, internal application. It is used only by the owner of EIMotorsports to manage the owner's own inventory and the owner's own eBay seller account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else has an account.

What the application accesses

When the owner connects the application to eBay, the owner approves access to data from the owner's own seller account: listings, orders, fees and sales analytics. The application uses this data only to show and organize it inside the application.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own database. The eBay authorization token is stored in encrypted form on that same computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect the application at any time from within the application, or remove its permissions from the eBay account settings.
